# Largest probability that X1+X2+X3 < 2X4 for iid X

## Description of constant

$C\_{92}$ is the largest possible probability that $X\_4$ is more than one and a half times the average of $X\_1,X\_2,X\_3$, for independent, identically distributed nonnegative random variables:

$$C_{92} = \sup_{\mu} \mathbb{P}[X_1 + X_2 + X_3 < 2 X_4],$$

where the supremum is over all probability measures $\mu$ on $[0,\infty)$ and $X\_1,\dots,X\_4$ are independent with law $\mu$. The question was posed by Tobias Fritz on MathOverflow [MO474916] and studied by Bellec and Fritz [BF2024]. From the third version of [BF2024] on, their Conjecture 5.1 states that the lower bound $0.4006953723\ldots$ is the exact value (the first versions conjectured $0.4$, which [Yu2024] disproved). The constant is listed as the "beat the average game", Problem 6.39, in the AlphaEvolve problem collection [GGSWT2025].

The value is now known exactly [ZB2026]:

$$C_{92} = \sup_{0<p<1} \frac{p(2-p)}{1+p+p^2+p^3} = \frac{r^3-6r^2+6r}{4} = 0.4006953723\ldots,$$

where $r$ is the root in $(0,1)$ of $r^4-4r^3-3r^2-2r+2$.

## Known upper bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |
| ----- | --------- | -------- |
| $1/2$ | [MO474916] | Symmetrization: at most two of the four versions of the inequality hold at once (Fritz, in the question, July 2024). |
| $7/15 \approx 0.4667$ | [Fri2024] | Six points: at most 28 of the 60 versions hold at once. |
| $0.422$ | [BF2024] | First version (Dec 2024). |
| $673/1615 \approx 0.4167$ | [BF2024] | Third version (Mar 2025): largest fraction of the inequalities that can hold simultaneously among 20 points (a bound using only exchangeability), computed by mixed-integer programming. |
| $0.40069634$ | [Tom2026] | Flag-algebra semidefinite program on nine sample points, with a Lean 4 proof (Oct 2026). |
| $0.4006953723\ldots$ (exact) | [ZB2026] | Four-point flag-algebra certificate over $\mathbb{Q}(r)$ that also records pairwise ratio comparisons with cutoffs $7/6, 3/2, 2$; 259 patterns, completeness by Gordan certificates; two independent exact verifiers; Lean 4 proof `BeatAvg.Four.C_eq_Bstar`. Additive comparisons only give $0.4025452$ (six points) and $0.4007545$ (seven). Data and verifiers: [github.com/antalez/flag-algebras-iid](https://github.com/antalez/flag-algebras-iid/tree/df94412) (commit df94412; also arXiv ancillary files). |

## Known lower bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |
| ----- | --------- | -------- |
| $0.3080$ | [MO474916] | Gamma distribution with shape parameter about $0.5$, evaluated numerically (Fritz, in the question, July 2024). |
| $0.343$ | [jle2024] | Bernoulli multiscale scheme. |
| $208/567 \approx 0.3668$ | [Fri2024] | $\frac12\delta\_0+\frac16\delta\_5+\frac13\delta\_9$ boosted by the scheme of [jle2024]. |
| $0.4$ | [BF2024] | First version (Dec 2024): the multiscale law $X=\sum\_{k\ge0}\eta^k Y\_k$, $Y\_k$ iid with law $p\delta\_0+(1-p)\mathrm{Unif}\\{1-2^{-j}: 1\le j\le N\\}$, with $p=1/2$, $\eta\to0$, $N\to\infty$. |
| $0.4006953723\ldots$ | [Yu2024] | Optimizing the mass at $0$ in a continuous variant of the construction (Dec 2024), adopted in [BF2024] from the third version on; the optimum is $p=r=0.4743464554\ldots$. |
| $0.389$ | [GGSWT2025] | AlphaEvolve search over discrete laws. |

## Additional comments

- Variants: replacing $2$ by a parameter $c$ gives $S\_3(c)=\sup\_\mu \mathbb{P}[X\_1+X\_2+X\_3<cX\_4]$, and similarly $S\_2(c)=\sup\_\mu\mathbb{P}[X\_1+X\_2<cX\_3]$. [ZB2026] determines both exactly on several intervals of $c$ and certifies many discontinuities. In particular $S\_3(c)<1/2$ for $c\le1+\sqrt2$ and $S\_3(c)>0.5034$ for $c>1+\sqrt2$, so no $c$ gives $S\_3(c)=1/2$ (Problem 5.3 of [BF2024]); for $S\_2$ the threshold is the golden ratio. Tables of current bounds are kept in the repository above.
