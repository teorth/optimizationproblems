# Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_5$

## Description of constant

For a 2-colouring of the edges of the complete graph $K\_n$, let $k\_5$ be the number of $5$-element vertex sets whose ten edges all have the same colour. The **Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_5$** is

$$
C_{89b} := \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\min k_5}{\binom{n}{5}},
$$

the minimum over all 2-colourings of $K\_n$, or equivalently the minimum density of monochromatic $K\_5$ in a 2-edge-coloured graphon. It is often written $c\_5$ or $m\_2(5)$. Erdős conjectured that the random colouring is optimal, $C\_{89b} = 2^{1-10} = 1/512$ [Erd1962]; Thomason disproved this for all $K\_t$ with $t \ge 4$ [Tho1989]. See [C89a](89a.html) for $K\_4$.

## Known upper bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |

| $1/512 \approx 0.001953$ | [Erd1962] | Uniformly random colouring. Erdős conjectured that this is sharp. |

## Additional comments

- Noam Feinstein announced $c_5 < 0.001652$ at a [Technion seminar on 21 January 2026](https://math.technion.ac.il/events/noam-feinstein/); the [RSA 2025 abstract](https://dmg.tuwien.ac.at/rsa2025/) credits joint work with Chaim Even-Zohar [FEZ2025/26]. This is an announcement with no public certificate we could check. Its displayed threshold is stronger than our round-3 $0.001653032$ and weaker than the round-4 candidate.
- [KPS2023] report that a flag algebra computation on $9$-vertex graphs suggests $C\_{89b} \geq 0.001557$ from the floating-point solver output, but did not turn this into an exact certificate.
- The upper bounds come from (weighted) blow-ups of finite 2-coloured graphs, or step graphons, whose monochromatic $K\_5$ density can be computed exactly; the lower bounds come from flag algebras.
