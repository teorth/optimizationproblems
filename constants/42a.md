# Turan's pure power sum constant

## Description of constant

The constant $C_{42}$ is $\limsup_{n\to \infty}R_n$, where
$$R_n=\min\max_{1\leq k\leq n} \left\lvert \sum_{1\leq i\leq n}z_i^k\right\rvert,$$
where the minimum is taken over all $z_1,\ldots,z_n\in \mathbb{C}$ with $\max_i \lvert z_i\rvert=1$.

## Additional comments

- Computational investigations by Cheer and Goldston [CG96] suggest that $C_{42}$ is close to $0.7$.
- $C_{42}$ is the optimal constant for [Erdős problem #519](https://www.erdosproblems.com/519).
- Certified upper bounds for $R\_n$ at 13 specific values $300\le n\le 10^6$ (for example $R\_{1000000}\le 0.686368028005$), from explicit tuples stored as exact certificates and checked by two independent programs, are given in [Bak26b]. For $n\le 55$ they reproduce the values of [CG96].
