# Positive sign uncertainty constant in dimension one

## Description of constant

Use the Fourier transform with kernel $e^{-2\pi i x\xi}$. For a real function $f$, let $A(f)$ be the infimum of the radii $r>0$ for which $f(x)\geq 0$ whenever $\lvert x\rvert\geq r$. The constant $C\_{93}$ is the infimum of $A(f)A(\widehat f)$ over nonzero, real-valued, even, continuous integrable functions with integrable Fourier transform and $f(0),\widehat f(0)\leq 0$. This is the squared positive-sign uncertainty constant $A\_+(1)^2$. Problem 6.11 of [GGSTW2025] uses strict inequalities at the origin. [Tom2026, Lemma 2.4] gives the same infimum with this convention.

## Known upper bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |

| $0.30349081$* | [Tom2026] | The square of the certified radius $5509/10000$ for a self-Fourier Gaussian mixture. Version 6 is a self-archived preprint with Lean proofs. |
| $0.303490059061597222$* | [BCK2026] | Exact rational bound $151745029530798611/500000000000000000$. The degree-16,384 rational Laguerre polynomial gives a self-Fourier witness, negative at zero and nonnegative for $2\pi x^2\geq 1906884279970892581/10^{18}$. The bound rounds this cutoff divided by $2\pi$ upward. The Lean theorem is `NextProof.projected_rational_upper`. |
