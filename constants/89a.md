# Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_4$

## Description of constant

For a 2-colouring of the edges of the complete graph $K\_n$, let $k\_4$ be the number of $4$-element vertex sets whose six edges all have the same colour. The **Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_4$** is

$$
C_{89} := \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\min k_4}{\binom{n}{4}},
$$

where the minimum is over all 2-colourings of the edges of $K\_n$ (the limit exists by an averaging argument). Equivalently, $C\_{89}$ is the minimum density of monochromatic copies of $K\_4$ in a 2-edge-coloured graphon. It is often written $c\_4$, $m\_4$ or $C(K\_4)$.

Erdős conjectured that the uniformly random colouring is optimal, i.e. $C\_{89} = 2^{1-6} = 1/32$ [Erd1962]; Thomason disproved this [Tho1989]. For triangles the analogous constant is $1/4$ [Goo1959], and $K\_4$ is the smallest complete graph for which the value is unknown.

## Known lower bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |
| ----- | --------- | -------- |
| $1/46 \approx 0.0217$ | [Gir1979] | As reported in [Spe2011] and [Nie2012]. |
| $1/35 \approx 0.02857$ | [Spe2011] | Razborov's flag algebra method. |
| $204603019/7112448000 > 0.0287$ | [Nie2012] | Flag algebras. |
| $1/33.77 \approx 0.0296$ | [GLLV2022] | A flag algebra computation with $9$-vertex subgraph densities, improving the bound $1/33.9739 \approx 0.0294343$ cited there. |

## Additional comments

- [VSJ2026] also lists a value $0.0301423$ attributed to B. McKay (no construction released), and a bound below $0.030139$ announced by Feinstein in a Technion seminar in January 2026 (no value or construction).
- All the upper bounds above come from blow-ups of finite 2-coloured graphs (often Cayley graphs) with vertex weights, whose monochromatic $K\_4$ density can be computed exactly; the lower bounds come from flag algebras.
- The analogous constants for $K\_5$ satisfy $0.001524 < c\_5 \le 2320651 \cdot 2^{-24} \cdot 3^{-4} < 0.001708$ [PPSS2024].
- Related: the diagonal Ramsey numbers ([C17](17a.html)).
