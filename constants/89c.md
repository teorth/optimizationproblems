# Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_6$

## Description of constant

For a 2-colouring of the edges of $K\_n$, let $k\_6$ be the number of $6$-element vertex sets whose fifteen edges all have the same colour. The **Ramsey multiplicity constant of $K\_6$** is

$$
C_{89c} := \lim_{n\to\infty} \frac{\min k_6}{\binom{n}{6}},
$$

the minimum over all 2-colourings of $K\_n$, or equivalently the minimum density of monochromatic $K\_6$ in a 2-edge-coloured graphon. It is often written $c\_6$. Erdős conjectured $C\_{89c} = 2^{1-15} = 2^{-14}$ [Erd1962]; Thomason disproved this for all $K\_t$ with $t \ge 4$ [Tho1989]. Bounds are usually quoted as multiples of $2^{-14}$. See [C89a](89a.html) and [C89b](89b.html) for $K\_4$ and $K\_5$.

## Known lower bounds

| Bound | Reference | Comments |
| ----- | --------- | -------- |
| $1/\binom{252}{6} \approx 3.0 \times 10^{-12}$ | [ES1935] | Averaging: every 2-colouring of $K\_{252}$ contains a monochromatic $K\_6$, since $R(6,6) \le \binom{10}{5} = 252$. Better Ramsey-number upper bounds improve this slightly. |

## Additional comments

- No dedicated lower bound for $c\_6$ appears in the literature. Flag algebra computations of the size used for $c\_4$ and $c\_5$ are out of reach at $t = 6$. General lower bounds of the form $c\_t \ge C^{-(1+o(1))t^2}$ are due to [Con2012].
- [DFL2012] also give $c\_7 \le 0.6869 \cdot 2^{-20}$ and $c\_8 \le 0.7002 \cdot 2^{-27}$.
